About the project

A light management experience with personality.

The goal is to create an accessible football management game that focuses on fun decisions, team progression and a friendly cartoon identity rather than heavy simulation menus.

  • Casual football club management
  • Training systems and squad growth
  • Cartoon-inspired interface and tone
  • Designed as an indie digital game project
